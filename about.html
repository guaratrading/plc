Our History

Founded in 2015 by Solomon Assefa and headquartered in Addis Ababa, GUARA Trading PLC has grown into a diversified Ethiopian company serving residential, commercial, and governmental projects across the country. We import construction materials, electrical hardware, plumbing and heating equipment, and provide supply-and-fix contracting and professional consulting services.
